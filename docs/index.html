From online sync to local access

Prepare online. Serve on your network.

  1. Sync online

    Prepare the catalog and extension assets on an Internet-connected system.

  2. Transfer the snapshot

    Move the complete, finished artifacts directory into your offline environment.

  3. Browse locally

    Serve the gallery on your network. Search extensions, choose a version, and get the VSIX.

Before you deploy

The online sync component prepares the local catalog. Some extensions have their own runtime dependencies that may need separate offline preparation, and not every extension is guaranteed to behave identically in every target environment - validate against yours.
